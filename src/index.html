Dinis Cruz: a career told by example

This site uses one person's public record to show how you can present your own work. It is for anyone building a personal site, rewriting a CV or starting a job search.

What this site is for

Most CVs are lists. Most profiles say what the owner thinks of themselves. Dinis does two things differently, and both can be copied.

A CV told as a story

He published his CV as a slide deck, and he asked people applying to his teams for a presentation about themselves. We describe his deck, sum up his argument, and give a 10-slide outline you can follow.

Read: A CV told as a story →

The power of recommendations

A CV is what you say about yourself. A recommendation is what someone else saw. We explain why everyone needs them, what a good one says, how to ask for one and how to write one for someone else. Dinis's own public recommendations on LinkedIn are the worked example.

Who Dinis is

  • He has spent thirty years in the UK as a security practitioner, a CISO for UK companies and a founder, with one UK company taken through to an exit. (diniscruz.ai/about)
  • He is a former OWASP Board member, organised the OWASP Summits in Lisbon (2011) and Woburn (2017), and created the OWASP O2 Platform (2010 to 2012). (diniscruz.ai/about)
  • He was CTO and CISO at Glasswall, and CISO at Holland & Barrett. (his essay on presentations in hiring)
  • He founded sgit.ai, sgraph.ai, MyFeeds.ai, The Cyber Boardroom, RiskMandate.ai and VoiceDebrief.ai. (diniscruz.ai/about, diniscruz.ai/building)
