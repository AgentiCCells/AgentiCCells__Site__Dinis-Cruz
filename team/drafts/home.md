# Dinis Cruz: a career told by example

This site uses one person's public record to show how you can present your own work. It is for anyone building a personal site, rewriting a CV or starting a job search.

## Who Dinis is

- He has spent thirty years in the UK as a security practitioner, a CISO for UK companies and a founder, with one UK company taken through to an exit. ([diniscruz.ai/about](https://diniscruz.ai/about/index.html))
- He is a former OWASP Board member, organised the OWASP Summits in Lisbon (2011) and Woburn (2017), and created the OWASP O2 Platform (2010 to 2012). ([diniscruz.ai/about](https://diniscruz.ai/about/index.html))
- He was CTO and CISO at Glasswall, and CISO at Holland & Barrett. ([his essay on presentations in hiring](https://diniscruz.ai/2025/06/08/using-presentations-instead-of-cvs-in-hiring.html))
- He founded sgit.ai, sgraph.ai, MyFeeds.ai, The Cyber Boardroom, RiskMandate.ai and VoiceDebrief.ai. ([diniscruz.ai/about](https://diniscruz.ai/about/index.html), [diniscruz.ai/building](https://diniscruz.ai/building/index.html))

Every fact on this site links to where we read it. The [Sources](/sources/) page lists them all, with the date we read them.

## What this site is for

Most CVs are lists. Most profiles say what the owner thinks of themselves. Dinis does two things differently, and both can be copied.

### 1. A CV told as a story

He published his CV as a slide deck, and he asked people applying to his teams for a presentation about themselves. We describe his deck, sum up his argument, and give a 10-slide outline you can follow.

[Read: A CV told as a story](/cv/)

### 2. The power of recommendations

A CV is what you say about yourself. A recommendation is what someone else saw. We explain why everyone needs them, what a good one says, how to ask for one and how to write one for someone else. Dinis's own public recommendations on LinkedIn are the worked example.

[Read: The power of recommendations](/recommendations/)
