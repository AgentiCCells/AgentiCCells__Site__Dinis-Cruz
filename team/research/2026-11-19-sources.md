### Why it works as a "CV told as a story" (from his own argument)

Source: https://diniscruz.ai/2025/06/08/using-presentations-instead-of-cvs-in-hiring.md, read 2026-11-19.

- The essay argues a CV is a record, not a story: a static list of titles and dates that hides how and why someone worked.
- It quotes his note on the deck that the slide format is "much easier to consume" than documents.
- A deck shows the person's thinking, not only claims: the format itself is a work sample of how they communicate.
- He applied the same idea to hiring: at Glasswall (2020, internship candidates asked to "do a presentation about yourself") and at Holland & Barrett (job adverts asked for a presentation with the CV, with HR agreement and an explainer video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_UDjTUubuls).
- The essay gives a worked example of a career changer from medicine hired as a security intern after a 10-slide deck (the person is named in the essay; we do not name them here).
- An Open Security Summit 2022 session, "Using Presentations instead of CVs and Creating Diverse Security Teams", is cited in the essay's sources (not opened).

So the site can tell the CV-as-slides idea in his own terms and link to the essay, rather than inventing a structure.
